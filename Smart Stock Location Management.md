Feature name:
Smart Stock Location Management

Short description:
A store-keeping feature that records the precise physical location of inventory items, such as the warehouse zone, aisle, rack, shelf, or bin where each item is stored.

Purpose:
To ensure that storekeepers can quickly determine where an item is located, improving organization, retrieval speed, and control of physical stock.

Main functions/activities:

i. Assigns a specific storage location to each item.

ii. Records the item's warehouse, aisle, rack, shelf, or bin.

iii. Shows the expected location of an item when searching for it.

iv. Supports organized placement of incoming stock.

v. Helps storekeepers locate and retrieve items efficiently.

vi. Makes physical stock counting and verification easier.

vii. Helps identify misplaced items during store inspections.
