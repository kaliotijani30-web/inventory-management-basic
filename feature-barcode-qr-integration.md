SKU Creation and Management

Feature Name

SKU Creation and Management

Short Description

SKU Creation and Management is a feature used to give each product in a store a unique identification code called a Stock Keeping Unit (SKU). The SKU makes it easier to identify and manage different products in the inventory.

Purpose

The main purpose of this feature is to provide a simple and organized way of identifying products. It helps store keepers track products, check stock levels, and find specific items quickly.

Main Functions/Activities

* Create a unique SKU for each product.
* Assign an SKU to new products added to the store.
* Store product details such as product name, category, size, colour, or type.
* Search and identify products using their SKU.
* Update product information when necessary.
* Prevent the same SKU from being assigned to different products.
* Use SKUs when recording stock movements.
* Make it easier to locate and manage products in the store.
