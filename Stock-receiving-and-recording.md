# Stock Receiving and Recording

## Short Description
Stock Receiving and Recording covers the process of checking and entering goods into the store when new items arrive.

## Purpose
It ensures that newly received items are properly checked and added to the store records.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Receive incoming goods.
- Check the quantity and condition of the items.
- Match received items with the expected order.
- Record the items and their quantities.
- Update the store record after receiving the goods.
