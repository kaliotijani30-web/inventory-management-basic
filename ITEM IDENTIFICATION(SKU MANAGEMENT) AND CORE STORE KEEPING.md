Item Identification (SKU Management) and Core Store Keeping

Feature 1 – Unique Identification for Each Product

Description:
The system assigns a unique Stock Keeping Unit (SKU) to every distinct product so that each item can be clearly identified and tracked in inventory. During item setup, key attributes such as category, brand, size, colour, model, and packaging are captured and used to generate or validate a unique SKU code. The system enforces a rule that no two products can share the same SKU and stores all item details in a central item master record keyed by that SKU, ensuring that different versions of a product (for example, different sizes or colours) are treated as separate items.

Purpose:
To eliminate confusion between similar products, improve the accuracy of stock records, and enable precise tracking, reporting, and reordering by SKU.

Feature 2 – Receiving and Inspection

Description:
This feature handles the process of accepting incoming goods, checking them against the purchase order, and recording valid stock in the inventory system. When a delivery arrives, the system supports matching the received items and quantities to the purchase order, inspecting goods for damage, expiry, or incorrect specifications, and noting any discrepancies such as shortages, overages, or defects. Once inspection is complete, accepted quantities are recorded in the inventory by SKU and a receipt/GRN is generated, while any issues are flagged for follow‑up with purchasing or suppliers.

Purpose:
To ensure that only correct, good‑quality items enter inventory, that stock levels are accurate from the point of receipt, and that there is a clear record of what was received and in what condition.
