Feature:
Intelligent Barcode-Based Item Identification

Short description:
A store management feature that uses unique barcodes to identify individual inventory items and instantly connect them to their stored product information, including SKU, description, category, and quantity.

Purpose:
To provide a fast and reliable method of identifying items while reducing mistakes associated with manually entering or searching for product information.

Main functions/activities:

i. Assigns a unique barcode to each inventory item.

ii. Scans and identifies items during store operations.

iii. Links each barcode to the item's SKU and product details.

iv. Retrieves item information quickly during receiving and issuing.

v. Reduces duplicate or incorrect item entries.

vi. Supports faster and more accurate stock verification.
