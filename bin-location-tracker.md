# Feature Name
Multi-Zone Bin-Location & Dynamic Slotting Tracker

## Short Description
A core storekeeping module that maps out physical warehouse architecture into hierarchical zones, aisles, racks, shelves, and specific bins, linking every inventory item to an exact physical coordinate.

## Purpose
To drastically reduce item retrieval time during stocking and fulfillment, optimize physical storage space utilization, and streamline cycle counts and stock audits.

## Main Functions/Activities
- **Warehouse Grid Mapping:** Creates a digital blueprint of the store or warehouse layout categorized by zones and storage tiers.
- **Real-Time Item-to-Bin Assignment:** Associates scanned items directly to specific bin coordinates upon receipt.
- **Bin Transfer & Relocation Logging:** Tracks item movements when inventory is shifted from backroom storage to front shelves or between bins.
- **Storage Capacity Threshold Alerts:** Triggers notifications when specific bins approach maximum volume or weight limits.
