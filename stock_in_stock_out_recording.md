Stock In and Stock Out Recording

Short Description

Stock In and Stock Out Recording is the process of keeping records of items entering and leaving the store. It helps the store keeper know the current quantity of each item.

Purpose

The purpose is to maintain accurate stock records and prevent mistakes in inventory management.

Main Functions/Activities

- Record new items received into the store.
- Record items issued or removed from the store.
- Update the quantity of available items.
- Identify the SKU of the item involved.
- Record the date of stock movement.
- Keep track of the current stock balance.
