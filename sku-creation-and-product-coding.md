# SKU Feature and Product Coding

SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Feature and Product Coding is a system for giving each product a unique identification code. The code helps distinguish one product from another based on details such as product type, brand, size, colour, model, or other product characteristics.

## Purpose

The purpose of SKU Feature and Product Coding is to provide each product with a unique identity. It makes it easier for store staff to identify, organize, locate, and track products accurately within the inventory.

## Main Functions/Activities

* Create a unique SKU code for each product.
* Assign different codes to different product types or variations.
* Record product information such as name, brand, size, colour, and model.
* Prevent duplicate product identification codes.
* Search for products using their SKU codes.
* Update product information when necessary.
* Use SKU codes when recording stock received, stock issued, sales, returns, and other stock movements.
* Help staff quickly identify products during stocktaking and inventory checks.
