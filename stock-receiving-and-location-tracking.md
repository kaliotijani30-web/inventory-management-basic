Feature: Stock Receiving and Storage Location Tracking
Short Description
This feature records goods as they arrive in the store, checks them against what was ordered, and tracks exactly where each item is kept (store, shelf, rack or bin). It also keeps the running quantity of every SKU up to date as stock comes in and goes out.
Purpose
Make sure the goods received match what was ordered and paid for.
Keep accurate, real-time stock quantities.
Help store staff find items quickly and reduce time wasted searching.
Reduce loss, misplacement and errors in core store keeping.
Main Functions / Activities
Record incoming goods against a purchase order or delivery note (supplier, date, received by).
Scan or enter the SKU and quantity of each item received.
Compare received quantity with ordered quantity and flag shortages, excess or damaged goods.
Record batch or lot number and expiry date for items that need them.
Assign each received item to a storage location (store, shelf, rack or bin).
Update stock quantity automatically after every receipt.
Transfer stock between locations and record the movement.
Record stock issued out and returns, with the reason.
Show current stock level per SKU and per location.
Alert when stock falls to the reorder level.
Keep a stock movement history (a stock card or ledger) for every SKU.
Support periodic stock counts and record differences between the counted and system quantity.
