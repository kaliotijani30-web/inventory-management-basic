# Feature Name: Expiry Date Tracking
**Short Description:**
This feature allows the system to record, track, and monitor the expiry dates of all perishable inventory items in the store.

**Purpose:**
To prevent losses from expired goods, ensure customer safety by not selling expired products, and help staff sell items with closer expiry dates first (FIFO/FEFO).

**Main Functions / Activities:**
- Record expiry date when new stock is added (Stock In)
- Automatically calculate and display days remaining before expiry
- Alert/Notification for items that are about to expire (e.g., 30, 7, 3 days before)
- Highlight or flag expired items as unsellable
- Generate expiry report for all items
- Support FEFO (First Expired, First Out) during stock out/sales
