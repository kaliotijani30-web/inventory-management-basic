# Feature: Stock Receiving and Storage

## Short Description
Stock Receiving and Storage is a core store-keeping feature used to record goods received into the store and ensure that they are properly stored and organized.

## Purpose
The purpose is to maintain accurate records of incoming goods and ensure that products are safely stored and easily located when needed.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Record newly received goods.
- Check received items against purchase orders or delivery documents.
- Record the quantity of items received.
- Assign products to appropriate storage locations.
- Update stock quantities after receiving goods.
- Identify damaged or missing items.
- Keep records of suppliers and delivery details.
- Make stored items easy to locate and retrieve.
