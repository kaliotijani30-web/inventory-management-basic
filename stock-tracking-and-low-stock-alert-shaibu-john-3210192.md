# Feature: Stock Level Tracking and Low-Stock Alert

**Group:** 1 - Inventory Management Software
**Topic:** Stock Control and Replenishment
**Student Name:** Shaibu John
**Matric Number:** F/Nd/25/3210192

### Short Description
A module that continuously tracks the quantity of each item in stock and automatically alerts the storekeeper when any item falls below its minimum reorder level.

### Purpose
To prevent stock-out situations, avoid overstocking, and ensure timely reordering. It helps the business maintain optimal inventory levels and never disappoint customers due to unavailable items.

### Main Functions / Activities
- Record all stock-in and stock-out transactions in real time
- Display current quantity on hand for each SKU
- Set minimum reorder level and maximum stock level per item
- Automatically send low-stock alert (dashboard notification/email) when quantity <= reorder level
- Generate reorder suggestion list with supplier details
- Provide daily stock balance report and stock movement history
- Allow manual stock adjustment with reason (damaged, expired, returned)
