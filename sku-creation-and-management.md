# SKU Creation and Management

**Matric Number:** F/ND/25/3210168  
**Group:** Group 1  
**Topic:** Item Identification (SKU Management) and Core Store Keeping

## Feature Name
SKU Creation and Management

## Short Description
SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Creation and Management is the process of assigning a unique identification code to every item in a store. The SKU helps the store identify and distinguish products based on details such as product type, size, brand, colour, or model.

## Purpose
The purpose is to give each inventory item a unique identifier that makes it easier to organize, locate, track, and manage stock accurately.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Create a unique SKU for each inventory item.
- Record product information such as name, category, size, and brand.
- Prevent duplicate SKU numbers.
- Update SKU information when product details change.
- Search for products using their SKU.
- Connect each SKU with its corresponding inventory record.
- Maintain consistent identification of products throughout the store.
